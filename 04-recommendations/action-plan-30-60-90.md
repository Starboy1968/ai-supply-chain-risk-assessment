# 30/60/90-day action plan

## First 30 days - stop the bleeding

| # | Action | Risk(s) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enforce callback-to-number-on-file and dual approval for every supplier bank-detail change; add first-payment hold | R01 | Controller |
| 2 | Brief AP, procurement and executives on deepfake/AI-written payment fraud; adopt "no approval by voice or video alone" | R01 | CISO |
| 3 | Publish a one-page AI acceptable-use rule with a "never paste" list | R02, R05, R09 | CISO + Legal |
| 4 | Turn on CASB discovery for generative-AI sites; start an AI tool inventory | R02 | IT Security |
| 5 | Put the AP AI agent pilot on read-only for payment fields with human approval on all actions | R04 | App Owner |

## Days 31-60 - build the foundations

| # | Action | Risk(s) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Roll out an approved enterprise AI tool with no-training and retention-limit terms | R02, R09 | CIO |
| 7 | Enable DLP rules on generative-AI destinations; block unapproved AI domains and extensions | R02 | IT Security |
| 8 | Build the AI dependency inventory: process -> SaaS -> AI feature -> model provider | R03 | Third-Party Risk |
| 9 | Add AI questions (see questionnaire) to third-party due diligence for new and renewing vendors | R03, R08, R09 | Third-Party Risk |
| 10 | Stand up AI use-case intake with Legal/Privacy/export screening | R05 | General Counsel |
| 11 | Start weekly AI-forecast vs. baseline accuracy tracking | R06 | Planning |

## Days 61-90 - make it repeatable

| # | Action | Risk(s) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Negotiate AI clauses into the top critical vendor contracts: change/deprecation notice, AI-feature SLA, fallback right, no-training, retention limit | R03, R09 | Procurement + Legal |
| 13 | Write and test manual-mode runbooks for AI-dependent planning and logistics | R03, R06 | Planning + Logistics |
| 14 | Red-team the AP agent with injected test invoices before any expansion | R04 | Security Architecture |
| 15 | Require documented human rationale and source links for AI-assisted supplier decisions; start monthly sample audits | R07 | Procurement |
| 16 | Set hash pinning, signed artifacts and an AI-BOM requirement for any in-house model work | R08 | Engineering |
| 17 | Launch the KRI dashboard and report to the risk committee; re-score all risks | All | GRC Analyst |
